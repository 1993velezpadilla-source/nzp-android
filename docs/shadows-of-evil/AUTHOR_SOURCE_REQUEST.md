# Request for editable Shadows of Evil (World at War) source

Target creator: Klevi Alushi  
Project: Shadows of Evil (CoD WaW)

## Why we are asking

We are building a non-commercial/open-source mobile Zombies project on top of NZ:P and would like to preserve and finish the geometry/gameplay work in the unfinished World at War Shadows of Evil remake without decompiling or re-uploading the creator's work without permission.

## What would help most

If the project still exists, we would like permission to use one or more of:

- the original Radiant `.map` source;
- custom source textures/materials that Klevi personally created and is able to license;
- original models/props created by Klevi;
- scripts created by Klevi that are his to license;
- notes about which portions of Morg City were unfinished;
- permission terms / attribution requirements.

We do **not** need permission for assets Klevi does not own and we would replace any Treyarch/Activision-derived commercial assets that cannot be redistributed.

## Draft message

Hi Klevi,

I found your Shadows of Evil remake for World at War through your ArtStation portfolio. I am working on a mobile/open-source Zombies project based on Nazi Zombies: Portable, and your unfinished SoE remake looks like the best fan-made geometry base I have found.

Rather than decompile or redistribute your map without permission, I wanted to ask whether you still have the original Radiant .map/source project and whether you would be comfortable allowing us to adapt the parts you personally created for a non-commercial mobile recreation, with whatever attribution/license conditions you prefer.

If some assets came from Treyarch/Activision or other authors, that is completely fine — we can replace those. The most valuable thing would be the editable world geometry/collision and any original scripts/notes you still have.

The goal is to finish the missing areas and implement the complete Shadows of Evil gameplay: Beast Mode, rituals, tram, Rift/Pack-a-Punch, swords, Margwa, Civil Protector, buildables, main quest/finale and the smaller Easter eggs, while keeping a clear provenance list for every asset.

Thank you for the enormous amount of work you already put into the map.
